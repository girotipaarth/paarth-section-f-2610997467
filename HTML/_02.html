5. College Terminology

Syllabus
An outline of the subjects and topics covered in an academic course.
Credits
Units used to measure the weight or workload of a college course.
Semester
An academic term that typically lasts about half an academic year.
Viva
An oral examination conducted by examiners to assess practical knowledge.
Assignment
A piece of academic work allocated to students to complete outside of class.
